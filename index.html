Build the molecule, not just the answer.

Enter a condensed organic formula. The page parses the notation, builds a molecular graph, checks valence, identifies basic functional groups, converts the graph to SMILES, and then asks OpenChemLib to generate the final chemistry-grade 2D depiction.

Current scope: common acyclic condensed formulas containing C, N, O, S, P, F, Cl, Br, and I; common branch notation; repeated parenthetical groups; single, double, and triple bonds. The parser intentionally flags ambiguous or valence-inconsistent input instead of silently inventing chemistry.
